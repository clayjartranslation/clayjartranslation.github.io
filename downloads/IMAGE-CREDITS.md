# Image credits for Clay Jar Translation downloads

Each book in the PDF and EPUB opens with a public-domain or CC0 image of its main story, a central figure, or a closely related scene.
The compressed files live in this folder and are used at https://clayjartranslation.github.io/downloads/images/.
Every image was checked against Wikimedia Commons `imageinfo` extmetadata (LicenseShortName, Artist, DateTimeOriginal, Credit) and, where needed, Wikidata death dates.
The underlying artwork is public domain in the United States and worldwide because the artist died before 1926 and the work was published before 1931, or the file is a CC0 / public-domain dedication from the creator (including CC0 museum photographs of 3D objects).
Only faithful photographs or scans of two-dimensional works (paintings, engravings, illustrations, manuscripts, frescoes, icons, stained glass) are used, except where the photograph itself is CC0 or PD.
No CC BY, CC BY-SA, GFDL, fair-use, Unsplash, or Pexels images are included.
Faithful photographs of two-dimensional public-domain paintings are themselves in the public domain in the United States.
