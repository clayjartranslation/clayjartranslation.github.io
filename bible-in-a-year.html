Bible in a Year

Read this whole Bible in 365 days, including the Apocrypha and the other extra books in this edition. Each day has a reading from the Old Testament, the Psalms, Proverbs, the New Testament, and those extra books. Each section is divided evenly over the year and finishes on December 31. Long chapters are split by verse when that keeps the days even. Labels follow this Bible’s book groups, so Jubilees, 4 Maccabees, and the other extra books are not called Old or New Testament. Today’s date follows Eastern Time (New York). On February 29 in a leap year, read the February 28 portion again, or catch up.
